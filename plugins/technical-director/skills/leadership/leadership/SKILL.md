# Leadership Skills Router

A directory of the leadership skills for managing a team and working across an organization. Use it to point the user at the right skill.

## How to route

1. Name the underlying need in one sentence (who is involved, what outcome).
2. Find the closest skill in the catalog below.
3. If it is **model-invoked**, just use it. If it is **user-invoked** (marked ⌨), tell the user to invoke it by name — e.g. "run `/technical-director:delegating-work`" — since it only fires when they call it.
4. If two skills fit, name both and let the user choose. If none fit, say so plainly rather than forcing one.

## Catalog

### Your team — people, coaching, culture
- **running-effective-1-1s** — run or prep 1:1s, skip-levels, and career conversations.
- **having-difficult-conversations** — prep feedback, performance, promotion-denial, or layoff/termination conversations.
- **delegating-work** ⌨ — hand off work with clear decision rights; stop micromanaging.
- **coaching-pms** ⌨ — coach and develop product managers; leveling and growth plans.
- **building-team-culture** ⌨ — team values, norms, psychological safety.
- **team-rituals** ⌨ — a team's operating cadence and named recurring rituals.
- **energy-management** ⌨ — your own sustainable performance and burnout prevention.

### Engineering org
- **engineering-culture** ⌨ — DevEx, clock speed, Conway's Law, engineering principles.
- **organizational-design** ⌨ — org structure, reorgs, team topology, centralization.
- **organizational-transformation** ⌨ — moving to a product operating model; change management.

### Across teams and up
- **managing-up** — manage your boss, exec communication, escalations and asks.
- **cross-functional-collaboration** — work with other teams; reduce execution friction.
- **stakeholder-alignment** ⌨ — secure buy-in and executive/cross-functional alignment.

### Decisions and thinking
- **running-decision-processes** ⌨ — decision memos, RAPID/DACI, one-way vs two-way doors.
- **evaluating-trade-offs** ⌨ — cost-benefit, build vs buy, opportunity cost, sunk costs.
- **systems-thinking** ⌨ — feedback loops, second-order effects, leverage points.
- **planning-under-uncertainty** ⌨ — ambiguity, hypotheses, experiments, contingencies.

### Delivery and execution
- **managing-timelines** — deadlines, milestones, RAG cadence, scope/change control.
- **setting-okrs-goals** ⌨ — OKRs and goals with anti-gaming guardrails.
- **post-mortems-retrospectives** ⌨ — blameless post-mortems, retros, lessons learned.

### Meetings and sessions
- **running-effective-meetings** — agendas, facilitation, decision meetings, meeting hygiene.
- **running-design-reviews** ⌨ — design critique / crit / feedback sessions.
- **running-offsites** ⌨ — team offsite, retreat, and strategy-offsite planning.

⌨ = user-invoked (invoke by name); the rest fire automatically.
